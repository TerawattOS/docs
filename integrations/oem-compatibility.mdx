TerawattOS is built to work with any energy asset, regardless of who made it. Rather than locking you into a single vendor's ecosystem, TerawattOS uses the TerawattEDGE universal protocol adapter to pull data from any OEM and normalize it into a single, consistent view across your entire portfolio.

## The Problem with OEM-Specific Monitoring

Every inverter manufacturer ships its own monitoring portal. SolarEdge has one dashboard, SMA has another, Huawei has a third — and none of them talk to each other. For operators managing a mixed-OEM portfolio, this means logging into multiple platforms, reconciling incompatible data formats, and building custom integrations just to see your whole fleet in one place.

TerawattOS solves this by sitting above the OEM layer entirely. TerawattEDGE connects directly to your hardware using the asset's native protocol, and TerawattOS normalizes all incoming data into a common schema before it ever appears in your dashboard. The result: one platform, one login, one view — regardless of how many vendors are in your portfolio.

## Named OEM Integrations

TerawattOS has pre-built integrations for the most widely deployed OEMs in the market today, including:

- **SolarEdge** — inverters and energy hubs, including SolarEdge monitoring portal bypass for direct Modbus/SunSpec integration
- **SMA** — Sunny Boy, Sunny Tripower, and SMA storage solutions
- **Huawei** — SUN2000 series inverters and LUNA2000 battery systems
- Any asset that communicates over **Modbus RTU/TCP**, **SunSpec**, **DNP3**, **IEC 61850**, or **OCPP**

Pre-built integrations mean zero custom mapping required on your side. TerawattEDGE ships with protocol drivers for these OEMs out of the box.

## Multi-Vendor Asset Registry

Every asset connected to TerawattOS is registered in a centralized, normalized asset register — regardless of vendor. From the asset registry you can:

- View all assets across all sites and all OEMs in a single list
- Filter by asset type, OEM, site, status, or commission date
- Attach documentation (datasheets, warranties, service records) directly to any asset record
- Track the full lifecycle history of each asset — faults, work orders, performance trends, and firmware versions — in one place

Because the registry is OEM-agnostic, you manage one source of truth for your entire fleet, not one per vendor.

## Asset-Agnostic Data Normalization

When TerawattEDGE reads data from your assets, it translates every OEM's proprietary data model into TerawattOS's common telemetry schema before streaming it to the platform. This means:

- Inverter output power is always labeled `ac_power_kw`, whether it came from SolarEdge or SMA
- Battery state of charge is always `soc_pct`, whether it came from Huawei or a white-label BESS
- Fault codes are mapped to a standardized severity taxonomy so alerts fire consistently across vendors

You write alerts, build reports, and configure dashboards using consistent field names — no per-vendor logic required.

## Adding a New OEM

When you onboard to TerawattOS, the Terawatt team configures TerawattEDGE for your specific asset mix during the initial deployment. Most integrations — including multi-OEM sites — are fully configured and streaming live data within **2–5 business days**.

The configuration process includes:
1. Identifying all asset types and OEMs at each site
2. Selecting the correct protocol driver for each asset class
3. Deploying and commissioning TerawattEDGE hardware on-site
4. Validating data quality and schema normalization before go-live
