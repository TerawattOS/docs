Terawattos gives renewable energy operators a single platform to monitor performance, automate diagnostics, dispatch field teams, and control distributed assets at any scale. TerawattOS is the cloud command layer; TerawattEDGE is the IoT hardware that connects your physical assets to it.

## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request access">
    [Contact our team](https://terawattos.com/request-demo) to provision your TerawattOS account and receive your TerawattEDGE pilot kit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install TerawattEDGE">
    Plug the edge dongle into your asset's RS-485 communications port. Most sites are live within minutes — no specialist contractor needed. See [Installation](/terawatt-edge/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provision to your account">
    Scan the QR code on the device with the TerawattOS mobile app. The dongle self-registers and begins streaming telemetry automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor your portfolio">
    Your site appears in the TerawattOS dashboard. From here you can configure alerts, set battery dispatch schedules, create work orders, and generate reports. See [Quick Start](/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Looking for integration details? Head to [Integrations](/integrations/oem-compatibility) for OEM compatibility lists, supported protocols, and the REST API & Webhooks reference.
</Note>
