Getting started with Terawattos is a structured four-step process designed to get your portfolio live as fast as possible. Most customers have their first site streaming real data within a week of receiving their TerawattEDGE hardware — no lengthy professional services engagements, no generic demos.

## The Four-Step Onboarding Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discovery Call">
    In your discovery call, the Terawattos team maps your entire portfolio: asset types, OEM brands, site locations, connectivity situation, and team structure. This isn't a generic product walkthrough — the goal is a scoped deployment plan tailored to your fleet.

    By the end of the call you will have:
    - A confirmed asset inventory and site list
    - A hardware order placed for the right number of TerawattEDGE dongles
    - A deployment timeline agreed upon

    <Tip>
      The more detail you share in the discovery call, the faster the deployment. Customers who come prepared with an asset inventory sheet consistently get live data on day one of installation.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Your Assets">
    Once your TerawattEDGE hardware arrives (typically within 2–5 business days), your team or a Terawattos-certified installer connects the dongles to your energy assets on-site. TerawattEDGE supports a wide range of OEMs and communication protocols out of the box.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Supported OEMs" icon="solar-panel">
        SolarEdge, SMA, Huawei, Fronius, ABB, GoodWe, and 10+ additional manufacturers via vendor-agnostic protocol support.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Supported Protocols" icon="network-wired">
        Modbus RTU/TCP, SunSpec, DNP3, IEC 61850, OCPP, and OEM-proprietary protocols — configured automatically at the edge.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    From grid-scale SMA inverters to Huawei BESS systems to EV charging stations, TerawattEDGE handles the communication layer so you don't have to reconfigure anything in your existing infrastructure.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure & Train">
    With assets connected and data flowing, you move into configuration. A Terawattos onboarding specialist works through the platform setup with your team in a single session — typically two to three hours.

    **What gets configured:**
    - Alert thresholds for each asset class (inverter faults, communication loss, performance deviation)
    - User roles and access permissions across your team
    - Notification routing (email, SMS, webhook) per alert severity

    **Role-based access control:**

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Operator">
        Full platform access — monitor assets, manage work orders, configure alerts, run reports, and control dispatch settings. Designed for NOC and operations team members who manage the portfolio day to day.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Technician">
        Mobile-first access for field technicians. View and action work orders, commission new TerawattEDGE devices, capture evidence, and view site telemetry. Full offline functionality.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Viewer">
        Read-only access for investors, asset owners, and clients. See portfolio performance, live reports, and health summaries — without access to configuration or operational controls.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Field teams are onboarded to the mobile app in a single session — the commissioning workflow takes under five minutes per device.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go Live & Scale">
    Your portfolio is live. Telemetry is streaming, alerts are active, and your team has the tools they need. From here, scaling is frictionless.

    - **Add new sites** in minutes by provisioning additional TerawattEDGE devices
    - **Expand to new asset classes** — solar, wind, BESS, EV charging, CHP all supported on the same platform
    - **Unlock deeper analytics** as your fleet grows — performance benchmarking, yield loss attribution, and cross-portfolio trend analysis become more powerful at scale
    - **Automate reporting** for investors and sustainability stakeholders directly from live telemetry

    TerawattOS scales from 10 to 10,000+ sites without architectural changes on your end.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Prepare Before Your Discovery Call

Arriving prepared to your discovery call compresses the onboarding timeline significantly. Gather the following information beforehand:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Site & Asset Inventory" icon="list-check">
    A list of your sites with asset counts per site — number of inverters, batteries, meters, EV chargers, and any other monitored equipment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="OEM Brands in Use" icon="tags">
    The manufacturer and model for each major asset type. This determines which TerawattEDGE firmware profiles are pre-loaded for your deployment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connectivity at Each Site" icon="signal">
    Whether each site has 4G coverage, WiFi infrastructure, or both. TerawattEDGE supports dual 4G/WiFi connectivity and buffers up to 30 days of data offline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team Structure" icon="users">
    Your NOC and operations team size, field technician headcount, and whether you have investors or clients who will need Viewer access to portfolio data.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
