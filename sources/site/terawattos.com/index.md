how we work

## How the Terawatt stack fits together

From solar panels in remote sites to executive dashboards, Terawatt connects physical energy infrastructure with real-time operational intelligence — closing the loop between the field and the boardroom.

01

#### Connect to any asset

TerawattEDGE IoT dongles plug into solar inverters, battery systems, and meters — communicating regardless of OEM vendor or protocol.

02

#### Stream live telemetry

Operational data is continuously collected, normalized, and streamed to TerawattOS in real time — then surfaced to every stakeholder in the right format.

03

#### Operate with intelligence

TerawattOS turns raw data into operational intelligence — automated alerts, health scoring, diagnostics, analytics, and work orders — giving operators full visibility.
