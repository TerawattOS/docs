# Privacy Policy

Learn how Terawatt collects, uses, stores, and protects information across our platforms, services, and infrastructure.

Last updated: 1 May 2026

## Who we are

Terawatt Renewables Ltd ("Terawatt", "we", "us", or "our") is the data controller for personal data collected through our website at terawattenergies.co and through the TerawattOS platform and Terawatt Edge hardware.

We are incorporated in Nigeria and operate across Sub-Saharan Africa. Our principal place of business is Lagos, Nigeria. For data protection enquiries, contact: privacy@terawattenergies.co.

## What information we collect

1\. Information you provide directly

- Account registration: Full name, work email address, company name, job title, and phone number when you create a TerawattOS account or apply for a pilot.
- Pilot intake form: Portfolio size, asset types, geography, and current monitoring tooling — used solely to assess fit and tailor your onboarding.
- Billing information: Payment card details and billing address, processed by our payment providers (Paystack and Stripe). We do not store raw card numbers on our systems.
- Communications: Content of emails, support tickets, and messages you send us.
- Feedback and research: Responses to surveys, user research sessions, and product feedback forms.

2\. Information collected automatically

- Usage data: Pages visited, features used, session duration, click paths, and error logs within TerawattOS — used to improve the product.
- Device and technical data: Browser type, operating system, IP address, screen resolution, and referring URL.
- Telemetry data: Energy asset telemetry (power output, irradiance, temperature, fault codes) streamed through Terawatt Edge or OEM integrations. This is operational data about physical equipment, not personal data, though it may be associated with your account.
- Cookies: See Section 10 for our full cookie policy.

3\. Information from third parties

- OEM cloud APIs (SMA, Huawei FusionSolar, SolarEdge) when you authorise TerawattOS to connect to your accounts with those services.
- Publicly available company information for the purpose of account verification.

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

- Service delivery: Creating and managing your account, provisioning TerawattOS access, and processing transactions.
- Platform operation: Ingesting, processing, and displaying telemetry data; generating reports; detecting anomalies; and dispatching alerts.
- Customer support: Responding to support requests, troubleshooting issues, and managing your onboarding.
- Product improvement: Analysing usage patterns to identify bugs, improve performance, and prioritise new features. We use aggregated, anonymised data where possible.
- Communications: Sending transactional emails (password resets, billing receipts, usage alerts) and, where you have consented, product updates and newsletters. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- Security and fraud prevention: Monitoring for unauthorised access, detecting abuse, and complying with legal obligations.
- Business operations: Invoicing, contract management, and financial reporting.

## Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR or equivalent data protection law applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

- Contract performance: Processing necessary to deliver TerawattOS services under your subscription agreement.
- Legitimate interests: Product analytics, security monitoring, fraud prevention, and direct marketing to existing customers. We balance these interests against your privacy rights.
- Consent: For optional communications (newsletter, research participation) and non-essential cookies.
- Legal obligation: Where required by applicable law, including tax, financial, and data retention obligations.

## How we share your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only as follows:

- Service providers: Infrastructure providers (AWS, Google Cloud), payment processors (Paystack, Stripe), analytics tools, and email delivery services all bound by data processing agreements.
- OEM integrations: Where you authorise us to connect to an OEM platform, we exchange data with that platform on your behalf. You control which integrations are active.
- Within your organisation: Account administrators can view and manage data for users in their TerawattOS organisation. We recommend you configure role-based access accordingly.
- Legal requirements: Where required by court order, regulation, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Terawatt, our customers, or others.
- Business transfers: If Terawatt is acquired or merges with another entity, your data may transfer as part of that transaction. We will notify you in advance.

## Data Retention

We retain data for as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with legal obligations:

- Account data: Retained for the duration of your subscription and for 2 years after account closure, unless deletion is requested earlier.
- Telemetry data: Retained according to your plan (Growth: 90 days; Enterprise: 5 years). You may export your data at any time.
- Financial records: Retained for 7 years in compliance with Nigerian tax and financial reporting requirements.
- Support communications: Retained for 3 years to facilitate ongoing support and dispute resolution.

You may request earlier deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations) by contacting privacy@terawattenergies.co.

## Security

We apply industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

- All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256.
- Terawatt Edge devices authenticate via x.509 certificates and communicate over encrypted tunnels.
- TerawattOS is built toward SOC 2 Type II compliance. Our security posture is reviewed annually by an independent auditor.
- Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel via multi-factor authentication and role-based access controls.
- We maintain a responsible disclosure programme. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to security@terawattenergies.co.

Despite these measures, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords and to enable SSO where available.

## International data transfers

Terawatt's primary infrastructure is hosted within AWS regions in Africa (Cape Town) and Europe (Frankfurt). Some service providers may process data in other regions. Where data is transferred outside Nigeria or the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable.

## Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights in respect of your personal data:

- Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
- Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
- Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- Restriction: Request that processing be restricted in certain circumstances.
- Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@terawattenergies.co. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask for identity verification before actioning a request.

## Cookies and tracking

We use the following categories of cookies:

- Strictly necessary: Session authentication and security tokens. These cannot be disabled without breaking the platform.
- Analytics (with consent): Aggregated usage analytics to understand how TerawattOS is used. We use privacy-focused tooling and do not share this data with advertising networks.
- Marketing (with consent): Tracking pixels on the marketing website to measure campaign effectiveness. You can withdraw consent at any time via the cookie banner.

You can manage cookie preferences via the cookie settings link in the website footer or in your browser settings.

## Third-party services

TerawattOS integrates with third-party OEM platforms (SMA, Huawei, SolarEdge, etc.) at your direction. When you authorise these integrations, the relevant OEM's own privacy policy also applies to data processed by their systems. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party OEMs.

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email (to the address on your account) and by posting a notice within TerawattOS at least 14 days before the change takes effect. Continued use of the platform after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

The version history of this policy is available on request.
