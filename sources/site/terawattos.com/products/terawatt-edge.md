## Live Telemetry

Terawatt Edge streams live telemetry from your device to TerawattOS every second. Every power reading, voltage measurement, and fault code is transmitted over a secure encrypted cellular connection and appears instantly in your TerawattOS dashboard.

- 01Sub-second data polling: Configurable polling intervals from 1 second to 15 minutes depending on asset type and operator preference.
- 02Full parameter coverage: AC/DC voltage, current, output, frequency, state of charge, temperature, fault codes, and custom registers — captured and normalized.
- 03Offline-resilient buffering: Up to 30 days of telemetry stored locally when connectivity drops. Synced in full when the connection returns — no gaps in history.
- 04Multi-protocol support: Modbus RTU, Modbus TCP, SunSpec, and custom proprietary protocols — one Edge dongle handles the full range of energy hardware out of the box.
- 05Encrypted in transit: All telemetry is transmitted over MQTT with TLS 1.3 encryption and mutual device authentication — no unprotected data on the wire.

## System Control

Terawatt Edge isn't passive. Through TerawattOS, operators can send control commands directly to connected assets adjusting output setpoints, toggling operational modes, and managing battery dispatch all in real time, without a site visit.

- 01Battery dispatch management: Set charge/discharge schedules, configure peak-shaving thresholds, and override dispatch modes for storage systems in real time.
- 02Operational mode switching: Toggle inverters between grid-tied, island, and curtailed modes. Activate emergency shutdowns or restart sequences remotely.
- 03Command audit trail: Every control action is logged — who issued it, when, what the parameter was before and after. Full traceability for compliance and incident review.
- 04Safe command execution: Commands are validated against asset operating limits before execution. Out-of-range instructions are rejected at the Edge layer — protecting hardware from operator error.
