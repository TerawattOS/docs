WHAT IS TERAWATTOS

TerawattOS is a renewable energy monitoring and asset management platform that eliminates the complexity of managing assets across multiple OEM systems. Instead of switching between disconnected inverter portals, battery management systems, meters, and SCADA platforms, operators can monitor and manage their entire energy portfolio from a single unified dashboard.

The platform consolidates and normalizes operational data from solar assets, battery storage systems, meters, inverters, and other energy infrastructure, providing real-time visibility, automated diagnostics, and portfolio-wide performance insights across every site.

WHY TERAWATTOS

## The energy market is fragmented. Your tools shouldn't be.

The world has surpassed 2 TW of installed solar capacity, yet much of the software powering it remains outdated reliant on siloed OEM dashboards, manual spreadsheet reporting, and reactive maintenance workflows. TerawattOS was built to modernize renewable energy operations at scale.

- 01Fragmentation: Each OEM has its own dashboard. TerawattOS unify all of them through a vendor-agnostic edge gateway.
- 02Manual reporting: 10–15 hrs/week building reports. TerawattOS cuts that by 90% through automated, scheduled reports.
- 03Poor visibility: Faults go unnoticed for hours. Our AI diagnostics surface root causes in under 5 minutes.
- 04Operational bottlenecks: O&M teams work from WhatsApp. We replace that with structured work order management.
