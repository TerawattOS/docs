## The grid is becoming a distributed system. Your tools must be too.

Utilities are absorbing thousands of rooftop solar systems, grid-scale batteries, and virtual power plants into networks built for centralized generation. The visibility gap is enormous.

- DER visibility gapsThousands of distributed assets with no real-time telemetry — making dispatch, forecasting, and curtailment management unreliable.
- Protocol fragmentationSolar inverters, batteries, and meters from dozens of manufacturers each speaking different protocols with no unified data layer.
- SCADA integration complexityConnecting distributed assets to existing EMS/SCADA systems requires custom integrations that are expensive and brittle.
- Regulatory data obligationsGrowing requirements to report on DER capacity, availability, and output to grid operators and regulators with no automated pipeline to produce this data.
