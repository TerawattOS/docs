## Your energy assets are working. Are they working together?

C&I facility managers often have solar on the roof, batteries in the basement, and a grid connection in the switchroom but no system that coordinates them intelligently.

- No unified energy viewSolar, battery, grid, and consumption data live in separate apps — no single view of energy flow across the facility.
- Missing ESG dataSustainability teams need granular renewable energy consumption data for Scope 2 reporting — but it doesn't exist in a usable format.
- Unoptimized battery dispatchBattery storage charged and discharged without intelligence — missing peak demand windows and time-of-use savings opportunities.
- No visibility into asset healthDegradation in solar output or battery capacity goes unnoticed for months, leading to underperformance and voided warranties.
