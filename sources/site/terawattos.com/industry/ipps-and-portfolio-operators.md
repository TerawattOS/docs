## Managing dozens of sites shouldn't mean dozens of tools.

IPPs and portfolio operators are running sophisticated infrastructure, but most are still stitching together spreadsheets, vendor dashboards, and manual reporting. The real cost is in the gaps.

- Fragmented visibilitySeparate dashboards per site, per vendor — no consolidated portfolio view makes performance comparison impossible.
- Reactive maintenanceFaults discovered after yield impact, not before — costing revenue and eroding investor confidence.
- Manual investor reportingHours every month assembling performance data from multiple sources into reports that are already outdated.
- No benchmarking baselineWithout normalized data across sites, it's impossible to identify which facilities underperform or why.
