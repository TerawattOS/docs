## Customer Success Manager

SUPPORT Full-Time Remote

Apply

#### About the Role

Terawatt is looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to design, build, and scale the core infrastructure that powers our renewable energy operations platform. You will play a critical role in developing the cloud services, data pipelines, APIs, and platform capabilities that enable real-time monitoring, diagnostics, and optimization of distributed energy assets across thousands of sites. 

As a senior member of the engineering team, you will help define platform architecture, improve system reliability, and ensure our infrastructure can scale with the growing demands of renewable energy operators worldwide.

#### Key Responsibilities

- Design, build, and maintain scalable cloud infrastructure supporting TerawattOS and related products.
- Develop and optimize platform services, APIs, event-driven systems, and backend architecture.
- Build and manage data ingestion pipelines that process telemetry from solar, battery, inverter, meter, and IoT devices.
- Implement infrastructure-as-code, deployment automation, and CI/CD pipelines.
- Improve platform reliability, observability, monitoring, and incident response processes.
- Collaborate with product, frontend, and edge engineering teams to deliver platform capabilities.
- Optimize system performance, security, scalability, and cost efficiency.
- Establish engineering best practices, architectural standards, and operational procedures.
- Mentor engineers and contribute to technical leadership across the organization

#### Required Qualifications

- 5+ years of experience in platform engineering, backend engineering, cloud infrastructure, or related roles.
- Strong experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure.
- Proficiency in backend development using languages such as Python, Go, Node.js, or Java.
- Experience designing and operating distributed systems, microservices, and event-driven architectures.
- Strong knowledge of containerization technologies including Docker and Kubernetes.
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure automation, and DevOps practices.
- Familiarity with databases, message queues, and data streaming technologies.
- Strong understanding of system reliability, security, and performance optimization.

#### Preferred Qualifications

- Experience working with IoT platforms, telemetry systems, or edge computing solutions.
- Experience in renewable energy, energy technology, utilities, or industrial software.
- Knowledge of SCADA systems, MQTT, Modbus, OPC-UA, or industrial communication protocols.
- Experience building large-scale monitoring or operational platforms.

#### What Success Looks Like

- Reliable and scalable platform infrastructure capable of supporting rapid portfolio growth.
- High system availability and operational performance across all customer deployments.
- Faster deployment cycles through automation and modern engineering practices.
- Robust monitoring and observability that enable proactive issue detection and resolution.
- A platform foundation that empowers renewable energy operators to manage assets efficiently at scale.

#### Why Join Terawatt

At Terawatt, you'll help build the technology infrastructure powering the next generation of renewable energy operations. Your work will directly contribute to improving asset performance, operational efficiency, and the adoption of clean energy worldwide.
