#### What types of energy assets does TerawattOS support?

TerawattOS is asset-agnostic across the distributed energy spectrum. It supports solar PV, wind, battery energy storage systems (BESS), Inverters, EV charging infrastructure, combined heat and power (CHP), and hybrid sites. If it generates, stores, or consumes energy and has a communication interface — we can monitor and operate it.
