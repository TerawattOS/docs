TerawattOS gives independent power producers and portfolio operators a unified command layer to monitor performance, manage assets, and report to investors from a single platform — whether you operate 5 solar farms or 500. Instead of logging into separate dashboards for each site and vendor, you get one normalized view of your entire fleet, with the analytics and automation tools to act on what you see.

## The Challenges TerawattOS Solves

<Accordion title="Fragmented visibility across sites and vendors">
  When every site runs a different OEM platform, there is no consolidated portfolio view and performance comparison becomes nearly impossible. Your team wastes hours each week switching between dashboards, exporting CSVs, and reconciling figures that were never designed to be compared — and even then, the result is a snapshot that is already out of date.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Reactive maintenance that costs yield and confidence">
  Without intelligent alerting and asset diagnostics, faults surface only after they have already impacted generation. By the time a problem is visible on an OEM dashboard, revenue has been lost and investor confidence eroded. TerawattOS detects anomalies before they become failures, so your O&M team can act while the asset is still performing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Manual investor reporting consuming weeks of effort">
  Assembling performance data from multiple sources into a polished report takes hours every month — and the moment it is published, the data is stale. TerawattOS auto-generates branded PDF and live web reports directly from telemetry, eliminating manual assembly and giving your LPs access to data that is always current.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="No benchmarking baseline to identify underperformers">
  Without normalized data across sites, it is impossible to identify which facilities underperform or why. TerawattOS normalizes generation data to kWh/kWp across every site — regardless of inverter brand or capacity — so you can rank performance, isolate outliers, and build a true benchmarking baseline for your portfolio.
</Accordion>
