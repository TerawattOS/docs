Commercial and industrial facilities with on-site solar, batteries, and EV charging need more than a monitoring screen — Terawattos gives facility managers the intelligence to actively optimize every watt. Instead of switching between separate apps for solar generation, battery state, and grid consumption, you get a single real-time view of your entire energy system, with automation tools that act on what the data shows rather than waiting for you to.

## The Challenges TerawattOS Solves

<Accordion title="No unified view of energy flow across the facility">
  Solar generation, battery state of charge, grid import/export, and facility consumption typically live in separate applications that were never designed to communicate. This fragmentation means you cannot see the full picture — and you cannot optimize what you cannot see. TerawattOS connects every asset through TerawattEDGE and presents a unified, real-time energy flow view on a single dashboard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="ESG reporting without usable data">
  Sustainability teams need granular, verified renewable energy consumption data for Scope 2 emissions reporting — broken down by source, time period, and site. That data is rarely available in a format that meets GHG Protocol, CDP, or local ESG framework requirements. TerawattOS captures it automatically from your assets and generates audit-ready exports without manual effort.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Battery dispatch that misses savings opportunities">
  Most battery systems follow a fixed charge/discharge schedule set at installation — without accounting for live tariff rates, demand peaks, or grid signals. The result is storage that charges at peak rate and discharges at off-peak, doing the opposite of what it should. TerawattOS automates intelligent dispatch using real tariff data, demand forecasts, and facility consumption patterns.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Asset degradation that goes unnoticed for months">
  A solar array losing 10% output due to soiling, shading, or inverter degradation rarely triggers an alarm. The generation figures still look reasonable — just not as good as they should be. By the time the underperformance is noticed, months of yield have been lost and, in some cases, warranty claims are no longer valid. TerawattOS establishes performance baselines and alerts you when output deviates — before it becomes irreversible.
</Accordion>

## Battery Optimization in Practice

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Peak Shaving">
    TerawattOS monitors your facility's real-time load profile and automatically discharges the battery when consumption approaches peak demand thresholds. This reduces your peak demand reading on the utility bill — one of the largest cost drivers for C&I electricity customers — without any manual intervention.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Time-of-Use Savings">
    The platform ingests your tariff schedule and programs battery charging during the lowest-rate periods (typically off-peak nights or midday solar surplus), then dispatches stored energy during expensive peak-rate windows. Every cycle is optimized for cost, not just convenience.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Solar Self-Consumption">
    When on-site solar generation exceeds facility load, TerawattOS directs excess generation into battery storage rather than exporting at a lower feed-in tariff. That stored energy is then used during non-generating hours — maximizing the proportion of your consumption that is covered by clean, on-site energy.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="EV Smart Charging">
    EV chargers are scheduled to draw power during solar surplus or off-peak grid windows. If the facility battery has spare capacity, charging is timed to avoid stacking EV load on top of peak facility demand — keeping your demand charge flat and your fleet charged.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## ESG Reporting: From Asset to Audit Trail

TerawattOS automates the full ESG data pipeline for your facility — from raw telemetry to submission-ready report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture at source">
    TerawattEDGE streams generation, consumption, and storage data from every on-site asset in real time. All data is timestamped, signed, and stored with a complete audit trail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Classify and attribute">
    TerawattOS automatically classifies energy flows — renewable generation, grid import, battery dispatch, EV charging — and attributes them to the correct emission categories under GHG Protocol Scope 2 methodology.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the report">
    Export verified renewable energy consumption data as a structured report aligned with CDP, GHG Protocol, or your local ESG framework. Filtered by date range, asset type, or reporting period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with confidence">
    Reports carry a digital audit trail back to raw telemetry. Your sustainability team, auditors, and regulators can trace every figure to its source — no black-box estimates, no manual adjustments.
  </Step>
</Steps>
