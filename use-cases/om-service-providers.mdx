O&M contractors using Terawattos dispatch technicians faster, close work orders with better documentation, and deliver on SLAs without the administrative overhead that typically consumes your team's time. Instead of chasing down fault histories, calling site contacts for access codes, or building client reports from scratch each month, TerawattOS puts everything your field team needs in one place — and keeps your clients informed automatically.

## The Challenges TerawattOS Solves

<Accordion title="Dispatching technicians without context">
  When a fault is logged and a technician is sent to site, they often arrive without asset history, a record of previous faults, or knowledge of what parts they might need. The result is wasted time on-site, repeat visits, and costs your business absorbs. TerawattOS auto-attaches fault history, asset documentation, and recommended actions to every work order before dispatch.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Paper-based site visit documentation">
  Paper records of site visits are impossible to audit, search, or attach to warranty claims. When a dispute arises with a client or manufacturer, your evidence is a handwritten form in a filing cabinet. TerawattOS captures photos, videos, technician notes, and completion timestamps directly in each work order — stored in the cloud, searchable, and audit-ready.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="SLA blind spots until it is too late">
  Without real-time SLA tracking, penalties are discovered retroactively — after the client relationship has already taken a hit. TerawattOS tracks response and resolution times per contract in real time, so your ops team sees where SLA risk is building before a breach occurs, not after.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Every site visit is a discovery exercise">
  Without live telemetry, field technicians cannot pre-screen a fault before travelling to site. They drive hours to discover a tripped breaker — or worse, return without resolving anything because the wrong parts were brought. Remote diagnostics through TerawattOS let your team assess the fault, confirm the required intervention, and dispatch only when physical presence is necessary.
</Accordion>

## From Alert to Resolved: How a Work Order Flows

<Steps>
  <Step title="Alert triggered">
    TerawattOS detects an anomaly — inverter underperformance, communication loss, or battery dispatch failure — and raises a rules-based or ML-driven alert. Smart deduplication suppresses noise; only actionable events generate work orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work order auto-generated">
    A work order is created automatically, pre-populated with the asset's fault history, maintenance records, technical documentation, and recommended diagnostic steps. The correct technician is notified based on escalation routing rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remote pre-screening">
    Your diagnostics team reviews live telemetry from the mobile app or desktop dashboard. They confirm the fault type, assess severity, and identify required tools and parts before dispatching anyone to site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Field dispatch and on-site resolution">
    The assigned technician receives the work order on the mobile app with full context. They complete the job, capture photo or video evidence, log resolution notes, and mark the work order closed — all from the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client reporting and SLA closure">
    TerawattOS automatically updates the client SLA tracker and can generate a client-facing completion report. Your account team has audit-ready documentation without any manual effort.
  </Step>
</Steps>
