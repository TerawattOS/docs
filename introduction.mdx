Terawattos is an integrated operations platform purpose-built for renewable energy — combining **TerawattOS**, a cloud-based management suite, with **TerawattEDGE**, a plug-and-play IoT dongle that connects your physical assets to the cloud in minutes. Together, they give you a single pane of glass across every inverter, battery, meter, and site in your portfolio, no matter who made the equipment.

## The Problem Terawattos Solves

Renewable energy operators today navigate a fragmented landscape: each OEM ships its own dashboard, reporting is assembled manually (costing operations teams **10–15 hours per week**), maintenance is reactive rather than predictive, and critical SCADA data lives in silos that never talk to each other. As portfolios scale from a handful of sites to hundreds, these pain points compound.

Terawattos eliminates that friction. One platform ingests data from every asset, normalises it into a common schema, and surfaces the insights and actions your team needs — automatically.

## Who Terawattos Is For

Terawattos serves every stakeholder in the renewable energy value chain:

- **IPPs & portfolio operators** — manage distributed generation assets across multiple sites and geographies from a single dashboard.
- **O&M service providers** — dispatch field technicians faster with AI-surfaced fault diagnostics and mobile-first work orders.
- **C&I facility managers** — optimise on-site solar, BESS, and EV charging to reduce demand charges and meet ESG targets.
- **Utilities** — integrate distributed assets into grid operations via REST API, webhooks, and industry-standard protocols.
