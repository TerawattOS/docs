This guide walks you through connecting your first asset to Terawattos — from unboxing a TerawattEDGE dongle to seeing live power data in your portfolio dashboard. The entire process takes under 10 minutes for a single site, and the same workflow scales to thousands of sites without added complexity.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A **TerawattOS account** — [contact sales](https://terawattos.com/contact) to get your organisation provisioned.
- A **TerawattEDGE dongle** — shipped to you after onboarding. Each dongle comes pre-configured with your organisation credentials.
- The **TerawattOS mobile app** installed on your phone (iOS or Android).
- Physical access to the asset's communications port (RS-485 or RJ-45, depending on the inverter or meter model).

<Note>
  TerawattEDGE stores up to **30 days of telemetry locally** if your site loses internet connectivity. The moment connectivity is restored, the device automatically syncs all buffered data to TerawattOS — no gaps in your historical record.
</Note>

## Connect Your First Asset

<Steps>
  <Step title="Unbox and connect TerawattEDGE">
    Locate the RS-485 communications port on your energy asset — this is typically labelled **COM**, **RS-485**, or **Modbus** on the device's terminal block or rear panel. Plug the TerawattEDGE dongle's RS-485 cable into that port, then connect the dongle to power via the included DC cable or USB-C adapter.

    TerawattEDGE communicates with your asset using whichever protocol the device supports — **Modbus RTU/TCP**, **SunSpec**, or any of 10+ supported OEM-proprietary protocols. No protocol configuration is needed at this stage; the dongle auto-detects the asset type during provisioning.

    <Tip>
      If you're a non-specialist or commissioning your first site, launch the **Commissioning Wizard** inside the TerawattOS mobile app before connecting cables. The wizard walks you through cable identification, port selection, and LED status checks with step-by-step photos matched to your specific asset model.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provision the device to your account">
    Open the **TerawattOS mobile app** and tap **Add Site → Add Device**. Point your camera at the QR code printed on the TerawattEDGE dongle's label.

    The app reads the device's unique identifier, authenticates it against your organisation's account, and registers it to the site you select — all automatically. You do not need to configure an IP address, port number, or DHCP reservation. TerawattEDGE establishes its own secure outbound connection to TerawattOS over **4G or WiFi** (whichever has the stronger signal).

    Within about 60 seconds, the dongle's LED turns solid green, confirming a live cloud connection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="View live telemetry">
    Return to the **Portfolio Dashboard** in the TerawattOS web app or mobile app. Your new site appears automatically, typically within 2–3 minutes of provisioning.

    Click into the site to see:
    - **Real-time AC/DC power output** (refreshed every 1–5 seconds)
    - **Energy yield** for today, this week, and this month
    - **Performance ratio** and capacity factor
    - **Asset health score** based on live diagnostics

    All data is normalised to a common schema regardless of the asset's OEM vendor, so the same dashboard layout works across every site in your portfolio.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your first alert">
    From the site view, navigate to **Alerts → New Alert Rule**. TerawattOS ships with a library of pre-built fault detection templates covering common failure modes — inverter over-temperature, string current imbalance, communication loss, grid frequency deviation, and more.

    Select a template, adjust the threshold if needed, and choose your notification channel (email, SMS, or in-app push). Save the rule, and TerawattOS begins monitoring immediately.

    When a fault condition is detected, the platform's **AI diagnostics engine** surfaces a probable root cause in under 5 minutes, so your team arrives on-site knowing what to fix rather than starting from scratch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scale to more sites">
    Repeat the same process for every additional site. Each TerawattEDGE dongle is pre-registered to your organisation, so provisioning a new site is always a single QR-code scan — whether you are adding your second site or your two-hundredth.

    TerawattOS scales to **10,000+ concurrent sites** within a single account, with role-based access controls so regional managers, field technicians, and investors each see exactly the data they need.
  </Step>
</Steps>
