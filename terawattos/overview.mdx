TerawattOS is the cloud software platform that sits above your entire renewable energy portfolio — consolidating and normalizing data from solar PV, BESS, inverters, meters, EV chargers, and other energy infrastructure into a single, unified operational dashboard. Instead of toggling between SolarEdge portals, SMA interfaces, Huawei apps, and manual spreadsheets, you manage every site, every asset, and every alert from one place.

## What TerawattOS Does

TerawattOS is the cloud command layer for your distributed energy operations. It connects to your assets through the [TerawattEDGE](/terawatt-edge/overview) IoT gateway — or directly via API — and normalizes raw telemetry into actionable operational intelligence. Whether you operate 10 rooftop solar sites or a 500-site utility-scale portfolio, TerawattOS scales to meet you.

## Why TerawattOS Exists

The world surpassed 2 terawatts of installed solar capacity — but the software managing those assets hasn't kept pace. Most operators still rely on a patchwork of siloed OEM dashboards, manual spreadsheet reporting, and reactive maintenance workflows. TerawattOS was built to replace all of that.

Here is what that looks like in practice:

<Accordion title="Fragmented OEM Dashboards">
  Every manufacturer ships its own portal. A fleet with SolarEdge inverters, a Huawei BESS, and a third-party meter means three separate logins, three data formats, and no cross-site comparison. TerawattOS unifies all of them through a vendor-agnostic edge gateway, so you see one normalized view regardless of what hardware is in the field.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Manual Report Assembly">
  Portfolio reporting consumes 10–15 hours per week for most operations teams — pulling CSVs, reformatting data, and building slides for investors and clients. TerawattOS automates the entire process, cutting report assembly time by 90% with scheduled, branded PDF and live web reports generated directly from telemetry.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Slow Fault Detection">
  In traditional operations, a fault can go unnoticed for hours before anyone checks a dashboard or a client calls to report zero generation. TerawattOS AI diagnostics continuously compare actual vs. expected output and surface root causes in under 5 minutes — before yield loss compounds.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Unstructured Field Operations">
  Most O&M teams coordinate via WhatsApp, phone calls, and paper checklists. There is no audit trail, no SLA tracking, and no visibility for clients. TerawattOS replaces ad-hoc coordination with structured work order management — every task is assigned, tracked, documented, and closed with a timestamped record.
</Accordion>

## Platform Scale and Reliability

TerawattOS is engineered for operators who cannot afford downtime on the software that watches for downtime on their assets.

- **Portfolio scale**: purpose-built for 10 to 10,000+ sites in a single account

- **Capacity managed**: 12 GW+ of renewable capacity currently operating through TerawattOS
- **Asset types supported**: Solar PV, wind, BESS, inverters, EV charging, CHP, and hybrid sites
- **Protocols**: Modbus RTU/TCP, SunSpec, DNP3, IEC 61850, OCPP

## Getting Started

New to TerawattOS? Start with the onboarding flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Book a Discovery Call">
    Work with the Terawattos team to map your portfolio, confirm asset types and OEM mix, and define your operational requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy TerawattEDGE">
    Install TerawattEDGE dongles at each site — typically a 2–5 day deployment window. Each dongle connects in under 5 minutes and begins streaming telemetry immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and Train">
    Set up alert thresholds, work order routing, user roles, and report schedules. The Terawattos onboarding team guides your operations staff through the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go Live">
    Your full portfolio goes live in TerawattOS. From this point, all monitoring, diagnostics, work orders, alerts, and reporting run continuously from one dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
