TerawattOS performance reporting eliminates the manual effort of assembling portfolio reports. Instead of pulling CSVs, reformatting data, and rebuilding slides each month, you configure your report types once — and TerawattOS generates them automatically, directly from live telemetry, on whatever schedule you set. For most operations teams, this cuts report assembly time by 90%.

<Note>
  All reports pull directly from live telemetry stored in TerawattOS. There is no manual data entry, no stale exports, and no reconciliation required. When you send a report to an investor or client, the numbers match exactly what the platform recorded — because they come from the same source.
</Note>

## Investor Reporting

Investor reports are designed for asset owners, limited partners, and financing institutions who need regular, reliable performance updates without requiring access to your operational platform.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Branded PDF Reports">
    TerawattOS generates a fully formatted PDF report on your configured schedule — monthly, quarterly, or as required. The report includes generation performance, availability, PR trends, fault summary, and yield loss analysis for the period. Apply your company logo, color scheme, and contact details so the report looks like it came from your team — not a third-party monitoring platform.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Live Web Reports">
    Share a persistent, password-protected web link that updates in real time from live telemetry. Investors can check current performance, review historical trends, and download data at any time — without you sending anything manually. The web report shows the same data as the PDF view, presented in an interactive format with charts and period-selector controls.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Stakeholder Viewer Access">
    Grant read-only viewer access to specific investors or asset owners. Viewers see a curated health card — generation totals, availability percentage, PR trend, and active alert count — without access to operational details, fault codes, or work order records. Viewer links can be set to persist indefinitely or expire after a defined period.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Yield Loss Analysis

Every downtime event in TerawattOS is automatically assigned a yield loss value — the energy that was not generated during the event window, compared against the weather-adjusted expected output baseline. Yield loss reports give you and your clients a clear picture of where generation was lost and why.

Yield loss is attributed across four categories:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **Fault-Driven Loss** | Generation lost due to inverter faults, string failures, BESS unavailability, or device-level errors. Each fault event shows kWh lost and revenue equivalent at your applicable tariff or PPA rate. |
| **Curtailment** | Generation curtailed by grid operator instruction, export limits, or inverter clipping above the site's export cap. Tracked separately from fault losses for contract compliance purposes. |
| **Grid Outage** | Generation lost due to utility grid interruptions. Isolated from site-side faults using meter-level voltage and frequency data — critical for force majeure and PPA contractual treatment. |
| **Scheduled Maintenance** | Planned downtime for preventive maintenance, cleaning, or inspections. Logged against closed work orders and excluded from unplanned availability calculations. |

## Report Scheduling and Delivery

Configure report delivery schedules from the Reports section of the TerawattOS settings:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Report Type">
    Choose the report type — performance summary, investor report, ESG export, SLA compliance, or yield loss analysis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Scope">
    Select the sites, asset types, or portfolio groups to include. You can create separate report configurations for different clients, asset classes, or geographic regions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Schedule">
    Choose a delivery schedule: daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly. For investor reports, monthly delivery after the close of each calendar month is the most common configuration. SLA compliance reports typically align to the contract billing cycle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Recipients">
    Add email addresses for report delivery. Recipients receive a direct download link to the PDF or a link to the live web report, depending on report type. No TerawattOS login is required for recipients of stakeholder viewer links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Branding">
    Upload your company logo and set your brand color for investor-facing PDF reports. Branding is applied globally to all investor and stakeholder reports generated from your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data Included in Reports

Every TerawattOS performance report draws from the same live telemetry record. Standard report data fields include:

- **Generation**: kWh produced by period (hourly, daily, monthly), total and by asset
- **Specific Yield**: kWh/kWp normalized production for cross-site benchmarking
- **Availability**: percentage of contracted generation hours the site was operational
- **Performance Ratio (PR)**: actual vs. theoretical maximum output, irradiance-adjusted
- **Fault History**: count, type, duration, and resolution status of all fault events in the period
- **Yield Loss**: kWh and revenue impact by loss category (fault, curtailment, grid, maintenance)
- **Benchmark Comparisons**: site performance vs. portfolio average and fleet benchmark
